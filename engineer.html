About my engineering

Me as an engineer

The strengths, perspectives, and experience I bring to engineering teams, as well as the areas I am continuing to develop and improve on.

What I bring to a team

Problem solving

I like breaking down complex engineering problems and using in-depth analysis to understand what solutions will actually work.

Technical thinking

My experience includes working with CAD, FEA, mechanical analysis, programming, engineering documentation, and real-world industrial projects.

Adaptability

Between school, design teams, and industry, I’ve had the chance to work on all kinds of problems and with different teams.

Collaboration

I value technical discussions and design reviews, and I’m always open to different perspectives and ideas when working toward a solution.

Strengths

Technical problem solving

In MECH 323, I worked on the fatigue analysis one critical location at a time, which included endurance limits, stress concentration factors, and a DE-Gerber safety factor at every keyway, shoulder, flange and retaining ring groove across three shafts.

Attention to detail

On the Trans-Northern obsolescence project, I consolidated field-collected equipment data which consisted of asset ages, nameplate information, and technical documentation of the assets into a central database where I resolved discrepancies directly with the client's engineering team.

Adaptability

During my internship, I regularly moved between different projects, tasks, and priorities. I had to quickly understand and adapt to new project requirements, learn unfamiliar information, and adjust to different ways of working which depended on the project and team I was working with.

Ownership

During my internship, I was responsible for creating and maintaining accurate engineering drawings for the Suncor bulk plant sites and ensuring the BOMs were properly updated for each location. I also played a major role in writing the final TNPI project report, which documented the project, its process, and the work completed so it could be referenced by TNPI for future obsolescence work.

Growing as an engineer

Areas I am actively working on as I move from being a student toward becoming a professional engineer.

Task Management

I am continuing to improve how I manage multiple tasks when several priorities come up at the same time. I want to get better at organizing my workload, deciding what to finish first, and balancing different tasks while making sure each one is successfully completed.

Technical communication

I want to continue to improve my communication skills of engineering information to both technical and non-technical teams.

Broader industry experience

I want to continue gaining more exposure to different engineering environments, so I can ultimately understand where my interests and strengths are a good fit.

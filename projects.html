Project 01 APSC 200/293 · 2024

Additive manufacturing of porous lattice structures for use in mouthguards

A six-week team design project: four candidate lattice geometries were modelled in SOLIDWORKS, screened with FEA, scored in a weighted evaluation matrix, then prototyped and tested. The prototype that came back from the printer changed the design.

  • CAD
  • FEA
  • Additive manufacturing
  • Prototyping
  • Design optimization

Project 02 MECH 323 · 2025

Gearbox shaft design & fatigue analysis

Forces and torques through a two-stage gearbox, bending moment and torque diagrams for three shafts, stress concentration factors at every keyway, shoulder, flange and retaining ring groove, and a DE-Gerber safety factor at each critical point.

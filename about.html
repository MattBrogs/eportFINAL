How I approach engineering

I like to think of the process as a repeatable loop rather than a straight line, which I learned from all of my design projects, work experience, and design teams.

  1. Understand
  2. Analyze
  3. Design
  4. Test
  5. Improve

I approach engineering as more of a balance between technical problem-solving and practical decision-making. I particularly enjoy understanding how individual components fit into a larger system, using analysis to make practical and smart decisions, and ultimately seeing how those decisions directly influence a physical or operational result.

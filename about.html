How I approach engineering

A repeatable loop rather than a straight line — most of what I have learned came from the steps after the first design.

  1. Understand
  2. Analyze
  3. Design
  4. Test
  5. Improve

I approach engineering as a balance between technical problem-solving and practical decision-making. I enjoy understanding how individual components fit into a larger system, using analysis to make informed decisions, and then seeing how those decisions translate into a physical or operational result.

I don't just want to design things on paper. I want to understand how they work in the real world.

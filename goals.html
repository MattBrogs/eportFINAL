Where I'm going

As I get closer to graduation, I am continuing to explore the areas of mechanical engineering that I am most interested in and where I can make the greatest impact.

My internship experience has made me interested in engineering jobs where I can ultimately combine problem-solving with a more hands-on type of work and truly see the results of what I’m working on and accomplishing.
